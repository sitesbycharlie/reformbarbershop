Your barber in
Donegal Town.

Men's haircuts, skin fades, beard trims and kids' cuts in the SuperValu Shopping Centre. A short drive from Mountcharles, Laghey, Ballintra, Lough Eske, Frosses and Inver.

How much is a haircut at Reform Barbershop?

A gents haircut is €18, student haircut €17, kids and senior haircuts €16, a head shave €16, and haircut and beard €24.

Do you take walk-ins?

We work mainly by appointment, but walk-ins are welcome when we can fit you in. The quickest way to book is to call or text 087 780 1057.

Where is Reform Barbershop?

We're inside the SuperValu Shopping Centre on Newpark Road, Donegal Town, Co. Donegal, with parking right outside.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 9:30am to 6:30pm. We're closed on Sundays.

Do you do skin fades and beard trims?

Yes. Skin fades, tapers, scissor cuts and beard trims, with our haircut and beard combo at €24.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, kids' haircuts are €16. Call or text ahead to book a time that suits.
